# The map pictures (`map.webp`, `map_2048.webp`)

`map.webp` (4096 x 4096) and `map_2048.webp` (the same map at 2048 x 2048, shown until you zoom in) are Grand Theft
Auto V's own map of Los Santos and Blaine County. They are Rockstar Games' and Take-Two Interactive's, not es_mdt's:
es_mdt's MIT licence does not cover them and gives you no rights to them. They are here only to be used with GTA V on
FiveM, where Rockstar's terms for the platform apply (https://fivem.net/terms), the same way WHIP's own map pack
(`whip_media_map`) ships GTA's map. es_mdt is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Rockstar Games or
Take-Two Interactive.

Don't want them in your copy? Delete both files: the Live map then draws a plain grid with every unit and call on it.
Or use your own picture of the same layout (4096 x 4096 px): put it in this folder, or on any https:// address, and set
`Config.LiveMap.image` in `mdt/config.lua` or in /whip → Settings → MDT → Live map (for example `'img/mymap.webp'`).
